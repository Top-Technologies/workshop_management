Workshop Management

Full workshop lifecycle for Tana Engineering PLC: Customer Request → Quotation → Job Order → Approval → Work Dispatches → Timesheets → Spare Parts → Quality → Acceptance → Invoice → Vehicle Release.

  • Configurable multi-level approval rules
  • Spare shortage → Purchase RFQ + returns
  • Quality checklist templates and e-signature acceptance
  • Job costing, profitability, and workshop dashboards
  • Two-way vehicle release: credit/trusted clients may collect unpaid with AR tracking; standard/new clients are blocked until the invoice is fully paid
